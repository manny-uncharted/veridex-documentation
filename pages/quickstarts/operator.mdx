# Operator Quickstart

Get operational visibility and control over agent activity in four steps.

## Prerequisites

- Access to a Veridex Developer Platform workspace (as operator)
- At least one registered app with active sessions

## Step 1: Review Spend Posture

Navigate to **Operate → Overview** to see:
- Active sessions and their remaining spend allowances
- Recent approvals and their outcomes
- Open incidents by severity
- Session health indicators

This gives you an immediate read on your workspace's risk posture.

## Step 2: Configure Policies

Navigate to **Operate → Policies** and set your governance rules:

| Policy | Default | Recommended |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Per-transaction limit | $500 | Set based on expected transaction sizes |
| Daily spending limit | $5,000 | Set based on operational budget |
| Approval threshold | $1,000 | Actions above this require manual approval |
| Chain restrictions | All chains | Restrict to chains you operate on |
| Token whitelist | All tokens | Restrict to assets you manage |

Policies apply immediately to all future actions.

## Step 3: Approve or Reject

When an agent or session exceeds a policy threshold, the action appears in **Operate → Approvals**.

For each pending approval, you see:
- What the agent wants to do
- How much it wants to spend
- Which policy triggered the approval
- The risk score

**Approve** to let the action execute. **Reject** to block it. If you don't decide before the TTL expires, the action is auto-expired.

## Step 4: Inspect a Trace

Navigate to **Operate → Traces** and select any execution to see:
- The agent's reasoning (what it decided and why)
- The proposed action (transfer, swap, etc.)
- Policy verdicts (which rules passed, which blocked)
- Signatures and transaction hash
- Duration and risk score

This is your audit trail — every action has a complete provenance chain.

## Ongoing Operations

- **Incidents**: Review policy violations in **Operate → Incidents**, organized by severity
- **Evidence**: Export audit bundles from **Operate → Evidence Export** for compliance review
- **Activity**: Cross-reference with **Build → Activity** for the full event stream
